Emergency information and advisories
Position

20.2°N, 84.9°E at 17:30 IST on 20 November, moving north at 12 kmph. 70 km south-south-west of Angul, 100 km north of Gopalpur, 110 km west-north-west of Puri.

IMD bulletin, 17:30 IST, 20 November

Intensity

A low pressure area over north interior Odisha, where it weakened at 05:30 on the 21st, after a well marked low at 23:30 over central Odisha, down from severe at 02:30 and cyclonic at the coast. IMD closed its cyclone bulletin series with that 23:30 update and gives no position since. The wind threat is over; the rain inland is the open question.

IMD all-India bulletin, 08:00 IST, 21 November

Track

Inland over south Odisha, heading north at 12 kmph. The centre crossed near 19.1°N 84.8°E between 07:30 and 08:30 and has run north from 19.2°N to 20.2°N since. The red warning on the south Odisha and north Andhra coasts now stands for rain rather than wind.

IMD bulletin, 17:30 IST, 20 November

Rainfall

Light to moderate rain over coastal Tamil Nadu and the Western Ghat districts on the 16th and 17th. Odisha expects widespread rain, thunder and gusty winds from the 18th.

IMD; Regional Meteorological Centre, Chennai

IMD observes every three hours, round the clock, at 02:30, 05:30, 08:30, 11:30, 14:30, 17:30, 20:30 and 23:30, and issues a bulletin after each. This page carries the latest observation it can verify; the time on the marker is the observation time, not the time of the bulletin. IMD's cyclone bulletin series closed with its 01:15 IST issue on 21 November and the all-India bulletin of 08:00 IST the same morning records the further weakening over north interior Odisha; neither gives a latitude or longitude. Last checked at 12:03 IST on 21 November. Thirty-eight fixes in the record.

Four more fixes: 11.0°N at 05:30, 11.2°N at 08:30, 11.7°N at 11:30 and 12.2°N at 17:30, when it became a cyclonic storm and took the name Akvan.

IMD bulletins, 16 November 2026

17 to 20 November

Severe from the morning of the 18th, at 13.1°N by 11:30 and 13.8°N by 20:30, moving north at 10 kmph, up from 3. By 05:30 on the 19th it was at 14.5°N, 85.3°E, moving north at 12 kmph, by 08:30 it had reached 14.9°N at 15 kmph, and by 11:30 it was at 15.4°N, 85.2°E, 430 km south of Gopalpur and moving north at 17 kmph. By 23:30 on the 19th it was at 17.1°N, 85.1°E, 240 km south of Gopalpur and moving north-north-west at 19 kmph, and by 05:30 on the 20th it had reached 18.3°N, 84.8°E, 110 km south of Gopalpur and moving north-north-west at 23 kmph. IMD expects the crossing between Narsapur and Puri, close to Gopalpur, by noon on the 20th, with winds of 90 to 100 kmph gusting 110. It crossed between 07:30 and 08:30, then ran north over Odisha, from 19.2°N at 08:30 to 20.0°N at 14:30 and 20.2°N at 17:30, weakening to a depression by evening and to a well marked low pressure area over central Odisha at 23:30, which IMD called its last update for the system.

Warnings and advisories

Sea and fishing

Fishermen are advised not to venture into the south-east and south-west Bay until the system passes. Sea conditions are rough to very rough, worsening from the 17th.

IMD bulletin, 16 November 2026

Report a problem

Someone is in danger

Call 112. Give the location, the number of people and what is happening. Do not wait to find the right department.

Water, power, roads, trees

Call the state helpline, 1070 in Andhra Pradesh, and your district control room. These are the numbers the collector's office acts on, and they are faster than anything this page can do.

Fishing boats and the sea

Fishing is warned against in the south-west Bay until 18 November and the central Bay from 16 to 20. Report a boat overdue to the coastal police or the district fisheries office.
